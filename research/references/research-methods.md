# Research References

## Web Search Tools

### Available Search Tools

| Tool | Capability | Best For |
|------|------------|----------|
| `web_fetch` | Fetch and extract readable content from URLs | Reading specific pages |
| `search` (via exec) | CLI search tools | Quick web searches |

### Search Best Practices

1. **Start broad, then narrow** — Search for overview, then specific aspects
2. **Use multiple sources** — Cross-reference information from different sources
3. **Check dates** — Prioritize recent information
4. **Look for primary sources** — Official docs, research papers, original posts

## Effective Query Writing

### Good Research Queries
- "What are the latest developments in [TOPIC] as of 2026?"
- "Explain [CONCEPT] and its practical applications"
- "Compare [A] vs [B] for [USE CASE]"
- "What are the pros and cons of [TECHNOLOGY]?"

### Poor Queries (Avoid)
- "Tell me about things" (too vague)
- "Everything about X" (too broad)
- Questions that can be answered without research

## Synthesis Guidelines

When combining information from multiple sources:

1. **Identify consensus** — What do most sources agree on?
2. **Note disagreements** — Where do sources conflict?
3. **Prioritize quality** — Peer-reviewed > blog posts > random articles
4. **Acknowledge uncertainty** — "Evidence suggests..." vs "X is proven..."
